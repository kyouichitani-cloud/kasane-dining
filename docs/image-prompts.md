# 画像生成プロンプト

このサイトの写真は、Codex内蔵のImageGenで新規生成したものです。3枚とも、冷たい青の空間、暖かな料理の光、深い赤の椅子を共通のビジュアル言語にしています。

## hero-kasane.webp

**用途:** ファーストビュー

**最終プロンプト:**

> Create a premium editorial restaurant photograph for a contemporary Japanese seasonal dining website hero. Horizontal 16:9 composition, no text, no logos. A refined cobalt-blue dining room at evening, subtle silver details, deep burgundy seating, warm pools of candlelight. In the foreground, an elegant plate of seared seasonal white fish with charred vegetables on a white linen table. A couple dining softly out of focus in the middle background, sophisticated but relaxed atmosphere. Cinematic natural lighting, realistic food photography, restrained colors, luxury hospitality campaign, ample darker negative space on the left for white headline copy. Avoid gold-dominated decor, beige palette, overly staged stock-photo smiles, visible brand marks, and illegible signage.

## course-wagyu.webp

**用途:** コース紹介

**最終プロンプト:**

> Create a premium vertical editorial food photograph for a contemporary Japanese restaurant website. A ceramic plate with charcoal-grilled wagyu sliced medium rare, seasonal green vegetables, root vegetables, and a restrained fermented sauce. Deep cobalt stone tabletop, cool silver highlights, a small warm light reflection, elegant negative space, highly realistic texture, fine dining without looking overly formal. Portrait 4:5 composition, no text, no logos, no hands, no excessive garnish, no gold or beige color cast. The image should feel cohesive with a sophisticated blue restaurant interior.

## open-kitchen.webp

**用途:** 店内・空間紹介

**最終プロンプト:**

> Create a premium wide editorial photograph of a contemporary Japanese open kitchen at night. Horizontal 16:9 composition, no text, no logos. Deep cobalt-blue tile and steel surfaces, subtle silver reflections, focused chefs working calmly, gentle steam and a controlled charcoal flame, burgundy dining chairs barely visible at the edge, warm practical lights against the cool room. Candid hospitality campaign photography, clean and quiet, realistic, cinematic depth, room for copy in the lower left. Avoid chaotic action, smiling at camera, gold-heavy luxury styling, beige walls, and visible brand names.
